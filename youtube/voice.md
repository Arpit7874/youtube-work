# voice.md

Every skill in this pack reads this file. Fill-in answers are in my own words (Hinglish), so scripts should sound like me, not like a template.

## Who I am talking to

Ek aisa insaan jiske 1000 se kam subs hain aur jisne abhi tak sirf ~9 videos banayi hain. Videos bahut kam bani hain, aur jo bani hain unse viewers ko na kuch seekhne ko mil raha hai, na unka entertainment ho raha hai.

Is problem ka mera hal:
- Videos zyada upload karo.
- Har video ki value jitni ho sake utni badhao.
- Hook ke liye thoda-bahut natural joke try karo, aisa jo scripted na lage, bas natural lage, taaki audience hook ho.
- Video poori dekhne ke liye rok ke rakho.
- Video ke end mein call to action dena mat bhulo, taaki comments zyada aayein.

Note: Ye answer mere apne channel ki growth ke baare mein hai. Mera naya page (niche neeche "My format" mein) men's grooming/health ka hai, wahan ka specific viewer abhi define karna baaki hai.

## How I actually talk

Ye meri live stream ke transcript se hai (sirf meri apni lines):

```text
हेलो
हेलो हेलो हेलो
सॉरी फॉर डिले ब्रो
आज पता नहीं यार कुछ तो ग्लिच हो रहा है मेरे

नया अपडेट आया तो मैं जैसे ही अपडेट किया उसके बाद मैं वेलो ओपन कर रहा हूं
तो लैपटॉप मेरा रीस्टार्ट हो जा रहा है
तो वेलो नहीं खेल पा रहे अभी
नेक्स्ट वीडियो आने वाली है ड्रैगन बॉल वाली
आज के लिए बस हो गया
```

Style notes:
- Seedha, bina formality, dost se baat karne jaisa ("ब्रो", "यार", "भाई").
- "मतलब", "बेसिकली", "तो" baar-baar aate hain jab kuch samjha raha hota hu.
- Tech words English mein hi (update, laptop, restart, glitch, stream).
- Ending chhoti aur friendly.
